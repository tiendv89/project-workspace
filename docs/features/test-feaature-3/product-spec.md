# Product Specification

## Feature
- Feature ID: `test-feaature-3`
- Title: Smart Daily Journal — AI-Powered Mood Tracker & Reflection Tool

## Problem
People struggle to maintain consistent journaling habits. Even when they do journal, they often lack meaningful structure or insight from their entries. Traditional journaling apps are passive — they store text but offer no analysis, pattern recognition, or nudges to help users grow. Users abandon their journals because they don't see tangible value from the effort.

## Goals
- Provide a frictionless daily journaling experience with a minimal, distraction-free UI
- Use AI to analyze mood trends and surface insights (e.g. "You tend to feel low energy on Mondays")
- Generate weekly reflection summaries that highlight recurring themes, wins, and areas of growth
- Send smart, personalized nudges that adapt to each user's journaling patterns (not just generic reminders)
- Allow users to tag entries with custom labels (work, health, relationships, etc.) and filter by them
- Export journal data as PDF or Markdown for offline access and portability

## Non-goals
- This is not a social or sharing platform — all entries are private by default, with no public feed or follower model
- No real-time collaboration or multi-user editing of journal entries
- No integration with third-party wearables or health platforms (e.g. Apple Health, Fitbit) in this phase
- No therapist or professional mental-health matching feature — this is a self-reflection tool, not a clinical product
- No gamification mechanics (streaks, badges, leaderboards) in this initial version
