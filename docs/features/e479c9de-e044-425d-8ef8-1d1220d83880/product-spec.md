# Product Specification

## Feature
- Feature ID: `test-feaature-3`
- Title: PantryPal — Zero-Waste Smart Kitchen Inventory Manager

## Problem
The average household throws away roughly 30% of the food it buys, costing a family of four over $1,500 per year. The root cause is simple: people don't know what they have, when it expires, or how to use it up before it spoils. Existing solutions (handwritten lists, generic reminder apps) require too much manual effort to maintain and offer no intelligence about what to cook with what's on hand. The result is repeated over-purchasing, forgotten produce, and unnecessary waste.

## Goals
- Let users quickly log pantry items by scanning barcodes or snapping a photo, with automatic product name and category resolution
- Track expiration dates and send smart alerts 3 days before an item is about to expire
- Suggest recipes based on ingredients currently in the pantry, prioritizing items closest to expiry
- Generate a smart shopping list that accounts for what's already at home — no more buying duplicates
- Provide a monthly waste report showing estimated cost and weight of expired or discarded items
- Support multiple household members sharing a single pantry with real-time sync across devices
- Allow users to set dietary preferences and restrictions (vegan, gluten-free, nut allergy) that filter all recipe suggestions

## Non-goals
- No grocery delivery or store integration in v1 — the shopping list is for reference only, not ordering
- No meal planning calendar or weekly menu scheduling in this phase
- No nutritional analysis or calorie tracking
- No restaurant or takeout suggestions — this is strictly a home cooking and pantry tool
- No social features — pantry data is private to the household, no sharing with friends or public recipes
