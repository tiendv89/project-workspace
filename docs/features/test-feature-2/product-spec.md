# Product Specification

## Feature
- Feature ID: `test-feature-2`
- Title: AI-Powered Plant Whisperer

## Problem
Millions of people kill their houseplants every year — not out of malice, but out of ignorance. Plants cannot speak, and humans cannot read their subtle visual cues (drooping leaves, yellowing tips, soil dryness) until it's too late. Existing plant care apps require manual journaling and generic schedules that ignore the unique microenvironment of each home.

There is no product today that uses a phone camera + AI to diagnose plant health in real time, surface actionable care instructions, and proactively notify the user before their fiddle-leaf fig gives up on life.

## Goals
- Allow users to photograph any houseplant and receive an instant AI-generated health diagnosis (overwatered, underwatered, root-bound, pest infestation, etc.)
- Provide a personalised watering and fertilisation schedule per plant, calibrated to the user's local climate data and home light levels
- Send smart push notifications — "Your monstera looks thirsty!" — triggered by AI-detected visual stress signals from periodic camera check-ins
- Support a plant library of at least 500 common houseplant species with care profiles
- Gamify plant care with streaks, achievements, and a "Plant Health Score" that motivates consistent care

## Non-goals
- Outdoor / garden plant support (scope-limited to indoor houseplants for v1)
- E-commerce integration (selling plants, soil, or fertiliser)
- Social / community features (plant sharing, friend leaderboards) — deferred to v2
- Offline-first operation — network connectivity is required for AI inference
- Professional-grade horticultural advice intended to replace a certified botanist
